Sustainability

Less pack. Better pack.

Corvel is a fictional brand, so this page makes no claims. It shows the kind of plain, specific things a packaging maker can talk about.

Right-size

A pack that fits its product uses less material and ships more per pallet. Sizing is the first conversation, not an afterthought.

Choose the material

Paper-based options where they suit the job, and honest advice where film is the better choice. Fibre and film can often be recycled where local systems accept them.

Mono-material thinking

Fewer mixed layers and fewer add-ons make a pack easier to sort and recycle. We can talk through the options for your product.

Ask us for evidence

Any claim on a real pack should come with documentation. Ask for it, and check it against the rules in your market.

Talk to us about your pack

Start with the brief and we will work through the trade-offs together.
